DeepLabV3
=========

Overview
--------

``deeplabv3`` runs a ResNet encoder at output stride 8 (the strides of ``layer3`` and ``layer4`` replaced by dilation 2 and 4) and applies atrous spatial pyramid pooling to its deepest feature: a 1x1 branch, three 3x3 atrous branches at rates 12, 24 and 36 and an image-pooling branch, each with 256 filters, batch normalisation and ReLU, concatenated and projected by a 1x1 convolution with dropout 0.5. A 3x3 convolution, a 1x1 classifier and bilinear up-sampling by 8 return logits at the input resolution.

Description
-----------

``deeplabv3`` runs a ResNet encoder at output stride 8 (the strides of ``layer3`` and ``layer4`` replaced by dilation 2 and 4) and applies atrous spatial pyramid pooling to its deepest feature: a 1x1 branch, three 3x3 atrous branches at rates 12, 24 and 36 and an image-pooling branch, each with 256 filters, batch normalisation and ReLU, concatenated and projected by a 1x1 convolution with dropout 0.5. A 3x3 convolution, a 1x1 classifier and bilinear up-sampling by 8 return logits at the input resolution.

The implementation reproduced is ``segmentation_models_pytorch.DeepLabV3`` (smp 0.3.2; the code path is identical from 0.2.0 to 0.3.4), because Shadrin et al. describe their models in smp's terms ("encoder backbone ResNet18, number of stages used in encoder equals to 3 ... default values for the other hyperparameters") and report smp's ``micro-imagewise`` metric reduction. It is ported to plain PyTorch with smp's parameter names, creation order and initialisation, so smp state dicts load with ``strict=True``; ``encoder_weights="imagenet"`` loads the ImageNet ResNet and adapts the stem to the input channels the way smp does.

The builder defaults are Shadrin et al.'s configuration: ResNet-18 with three encoder stages and 58 input channels (the 3-day horizon), on 32 x 32 tiles. With three stages the ASPP runs on the 128-channel, stride-8 output of ``layer2``; smp still builds (and dilates) ``layer3`` and ``layer4``, whose 10,493,440 parameters are kept for state-dict compatibility although the forward pass never uses them. The ``DeepLabV3`` class itself defaults to smp's ResNet-34 with five stages.

Notes
-----

- smp's DeepLabV3 differs from the paper's network: it keeps output stride 8 throughout (the paper trains at 16 with rates 6/12/18 and evaluates at 8 with 12/24/36), has no multi-grid, dilates every convolution of the dilated stages including the first block's (torchvision keeps the previous dilation there), adds a 3x3 convolution after the ASPP and dropout 0.5 in the projection (both from torchvision), uses ResNet-18/34 rather than ResNet-50/101, and up-samples the logits with align_corners=True.

- On Shadrin et al.'s 32 x 32 tiles with three encoder stages the ASPP sees 4 x 4 features, so the rate-12/24/36 branches reach only padding outside their centre tap and act as 1x1 convolutions (the degenerate case discussed in the DeepLabV3 paper).

- smp 0.3.x never calls its initialize() for DeepLabV3, so the decoder and head keep PyTorch's default initialisation; the port does the same.
- The image-pooling branch has batch normalisation on a 1 x 1 map, so training needs a batch size above 1, as in smp and torchvision.
- Height and width must be divisible by 8. Encoders: resnet18 and resnet34. smp's auxiliary classification head (aux_params) and output activation are not ported.
- This is DeepLabV3, not DeepLabV3+; no wildfire paper in the PyHazards inventory used V3+.
