Synthetic TCIF-fusion Inputs
============================

Random inputs in the TCIF-fusion layout (ERA5 U, V, W, SST, ALL, 30 history features, infrared images; channels last) for smoke tests of tcif_fusion.

Overview
--------

Synthetic data. Every value is a random number; nothing in it describes real tropical cyclones and scores on it mean nothing. It exists so that smoke tests and CI can run tcif_fusion and the cyclone benchmark; the paper's inputs (CMA best track, ERA5, GridSat-B1) have no PyHazards reader yet.

Data Characteristics
--------------------

- u, v, w (N, grid, grid, 5, 4), sst (N, grid, grid, 5, 1), all (their concatenation, 65 channels), his (N, 30), ir (N, ir_size, ir_size, 5); targets uniform 10-70 m/s. The defaults are the paper's sizes (grid 25, IR 224); the smoke config uses grid 9 and IR 32 with a small model.

Typical Use Cases
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

- Smoke tests and CI runs of tcif_fusion and of the cyclone benchmark.
