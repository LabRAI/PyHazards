Wildfire Risk to Communities: Housing Density
=============================================

USDA Forest Service 30 m rasters of housing-unit density, exposure and wildfire risk for populated areas of the United States (2nd edition, 2024).

Overview
--------

The Wildfire Risk to Communities populated-areas data set (RDS-2020-0060-2) maps where people and homes are and how exposed they are to wildfire, combining census housing and population estimates with building footprints and the Forest Service wildfire hazard layers.

Its housing-unit density layer (HUDen) gives housing units per square kilometre at 30 m, an exposure covariate for wildfire-risk and wildland-urban-interface studies.

Data Characteristics
--------------------

- Ten layers: BuildingCount, BuildingDensity, BuildingCover, PopCount, PopDen, HUCount, HUDen (housing units per square kilometre), HUExposure, HUImpact and HURisk.
- HUExposure, HUImpact and HURisk are NoData outside populated areas.
- The 1st edition (RDS-2020-0060) used 2018 Census estimates; the 2nd edition uses 2020 housing estimates.

Typical Use Cases
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

- Housing exposure and wildland-urban-interface covariates for wildfire models.
- Weighting wildfire hazard maps by the homes at risk.
