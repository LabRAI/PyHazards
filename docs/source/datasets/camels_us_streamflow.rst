CAMELS-US
=========

Reader for a local copy of CAMELS-US (671 US catchments, daily forcings, USGS streamflow and catchment attributes) in the setup of Kratzert et al. (2019), as NeuralHydrology reads it.

Overview
--------

CAMELS-US provides basin-averaged daily meteorological forcings (Daymet, Maurer, NLDAS), USGS streamflow and catchment attributes (Newman et al., HESS 2015; Addor et al., HESS 2017; CC BY 4.0, Zenodo 15529996). Kratzert et al. (HESS 2019) used 531 of its basins with the extended Maurer forcings (daily minimum and maximum temperature added; HydroShare, doi 10.4211/hs.17c896843cf940339c3c3496d0c1c077) and 27 attributes, the standard benchmark for the NeuralHydrology models.

``camels_us_streamflow`` reads that layout like NeuralHydrology's ``CamelsUS`` dataset (discharge from cubic feet per second to mm/day with the area of the forcing-file header, negative values to NaN) and builds streamflow samples. Defaults are the paper's: forcings ``maurer_extended``, inputs prcp, srad, tmax, tmin, vp, the 27 attributes, 270-day windows, train 1999-10-01 to 2008-09-30 and test 1989-10-01 to 1999-09-30 (validation 1980-10-01 to 1989-09-30, from NeuralHydrology's example configuration). PyHazards downloads nothing.

Data Characteristics
--------------------

- Layout: ``basin_mean_forcing/<forcing>/[<huc>/]<basin>_*_forcing_leap.txt``, ``usgs_streamflow/[<huc>/]<basin>_streamflow_qc.txt``, ``camels_attributes_v2.0/camels_*.txt``.
- Target ``QObs(mm/d)``; with several forcing products the input columns get a ``_<forcing>`` suffix.
- Normalisation and sample selection as for Caravan (training statistics; training samples with missing inputs or targets dropped; every evaluation day kept).

Typical Use Cases
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

- Reproducing the Kratzert et al. (2019) LSTM and EA-LSTM benchmark.
- Per-basin NSE / KGE / FHV / FMS / FLV evaluation with the flood benchmark (``flood.streamflow``).

PyHazards Usage
---------------

Point ``data_dir`` at the CAMELS-US root (with ``basin_mean_forcing/maurer_extended`` added) and pass the basin list.

Score models with ``BenchmarkRunner`` on the ``flood`` benchmark (task ``flood.streamflow``).
