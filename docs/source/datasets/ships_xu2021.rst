SHIPS Predictors (Xu et al. 2021)
=================================

The standardised SHIPS predictor table of Xu et al. (2021) with 24-hour intensity-change targets, read into the official leave-one-year-out folds.

Overview
--------

Each row holds the SHIPS predictors of one storm at one forecast time, standardised by the reanalysis standard deviation, with ``name``, ``year``, ``basin``, ``type`` (``rea`` reanalysis predictors or ``opr`` operational predictors) and the target ``dvs24``, the 24-hour change of the maximum sustained wind in knots.

``ships_xu2021`` selects the 121 predictors of the paper's 24-hour model (``SHIPS_PREDICTORS``) and builds the fold of ``leave_out_year`` as the official ``load_loyo_data`` does for the MLP: training rows are reanalysis rows except Atlantic rows of that year, test rows are that year's operational rows, and a random 10 % of the training rows (seeded) is the validation split.

Data Characteristics
--------------------

- Inputs (samples, 121) float32 standardised predictors in the official order; targets (samples,) dvs24 in knots.
- Split metadata gives each row's year (``groups``), storm name and basin; the benchmark reports MAE per test year.
- Rows with a missing predictor or target are dropped (``drop_missing``).

Notes
-----

- The official loyo_testing.py reads a ``..._w2020.csv`` variant that is not in the Zenodo record. The independent 2019 / 2020 operational files of the record are not standardised and are not read.
- PyHazards never redistributes the table; tests use ten real rows cut from it (tests/fixtures/ships_xu2021).
