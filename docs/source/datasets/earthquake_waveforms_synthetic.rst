Synthetic Phase-Picking Waveforms
=================================

Synthetic three-component windows with P and S wavelets for picking smoke runs. Not real data.

Overview
--------

This dataset is synthetic. Each event window is Gaussian background noise plus two damped sinusoids that start at the P arrival (about 8 Hz, strongest on Z) and at the S arrival (about 4 Hz, strongest on N and E); about a quarter of the windows are noise only. It exists to exercise the picking models and the ``earthquake.picking`` benchmark in CI; numbers obtained on it say nothing about real seismic data.

Data Characteristics
--------------------

- Inputs ``(n, 3, length)`` in Z, N, E order, 100 Hz, ``length`` 6000 by default (60 s, EQTransformer's window).
- Targets ``(n, 2)``: P and S arrival samples, NaN for noise windows.
- Deterministic: the same ``seed`` and sizes give the same data; ``micro=True`` makes 24 windows.

Typical Use Cases
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

- Smoke tests of PhaseNet, EQTransformer, GPD and the picking evaluator.
