NDFD
====

NOAA NDFD grids and warning bulletins used for wildfire forecast context, watches, warnings, and advisories.

Overview
--------

The National Digital Forecast Database packages official National Weather Service forecast grids and public hazard products.

In PyHazards it provides operational forecast layers and warning context for wildfire-weather workflows, including critical fire weather and watches/warnings fields.

--------------------
