NDFD
====

The National Weather Service's gridded official forecasts, including relative humidity, wind, Red Flag Warnings and SPC fire-weather outlook grids, archived since 2004.

Overview
--------

The National Digital Forecast Database holds the official National Weather Service forecasts as grids: temperature, humidity, wind, precipitation, sky cover, weather type and hazard (watch / warning / advisory) elements, written by the forecast offices and national centres.

For wildfire work it carries the fire-weather ingredients forecasters use (minimum relative humidity, wind and gusts) and the SPC fire-weather outlook grids.

Data Characteristics
--------------------

- Fire-relevant elements: SPC fire-weather outlooks (critfireo, critical and extremely critical areas, days 1-7; dryfireo, dry thunderstorm areas), minrh / maxrh / rhm, wspd / wdir / wgust, and the wwa hazard grid, whose codes include Red Flag Warning (FW.W) and Fire Weather Watch (FW.A). The SPC outlook grids became operational on 14 July 2011 and cover CONUS only.
- Archive access depends on the period: NCEI holds data from 6 June 2004 (older years in bulk archives, recent years online) and AWS from 16 April 2020.
- Some GRIB2 decoders misread the grid scanning order; NOAA recommends degrib, wgrib2 or grib2io.

Typical Use Cases
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

- Operational fire-weather forecast covariates and Red Flag Warning history.
- Comparing machine-learning forecasts with the official forecast.
