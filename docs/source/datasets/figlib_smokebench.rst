FIgLib SmokeBench
=================

The SmokeBench evaluation images -- HPWREN FIgLib camera frames with the SmokeyNet smoke boxes -- for prompting multimodal LLMs to classify and localize early wildfire smoke.

Overview
--------

SmokeBench (Qi, Li & Barnes, WACV 2026) evaluates multimodal LLMs on 5,046 smoke images with ground-truth boxes and 1,000 smoke-free images from FIgLib, the HPWREN Fire Ignition images Library of fixed-view camera sequences around wildfire ignitions.

SmokeBench released no data or image lists. The 5,046 boxes are the bbox_labels of SmokeyNet's metadata.pkl (Apache-2.0 repository, pinned and checksummed): one box per frame on 145 sequences, whose area quintiles are exactly the SmokeBench area bins (42, 4356, 11232, 28565, 83157, 2232020 pixels).

The frames are downloaded from the official HPWREN CDN on demand; PyHazards redistributes no images.

Data Characteristics
--------------------

- Smoke images are frames at or after the first visible plume (file names <timestamp>_+<seconds>.jpg) that SmokeyNet's annotators boxed; smoke-free images are frames before it (<timestamp>_-<seconds>.jpg).
- Frame sizes: 2,846 at 2048 x 1536, 1,994 at 3072 x 2048, 172 at 1600 x 1200 and 34 at 2560 x 1920 among the smoke images.
- Boxes are [x1, y1, x2, y2] integer pixel coordinates of the original frame (extremes of the annotated polygon); smoke areas range from 42 to 2,232,020 pixels.
- SmokeBench's 1,000 smoke-free frames were an unseeded random draw over all FIgLib sequences and were not released; the adapter draws a seeded sample from the pre-ignition frames of the 145 annotated sequences (5,666 candidates).

Typical Use Cases
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

- Zero-shot evaluation of multimodal LLMs on smoke classification, tile and grid localization and box detection (SmokeBench).
- Studying how smoke size and contrast limit early wildfire smoke detection.

PyHazards Usage
---------------

pyhazards.datasets.figlib_smokebench.FIgLibSmokeBench(root) yields SmokeSample(image_id, path, label, boxes) and decodes frames to RGB arrays; download_smokebench(root) fetches the boxes and frames.

pyhazards.prompted.evaluate_smokebench runs the SmokeBench tasks on it with a prompted multimodal-LLM backend (see the Prompted VLM Baselines page).

This dataset is currently documented as an external or inspection-first
source rather than a public ``load_dataset(...)`` entrypoint.

Inspection Workflow
-------------------

Use the documented inspection path below to validate local files before training or analysis.

.. code-block:: bash

   python -m pyhazards.datasets.figlib_smokebench.inspection --root /path/to/figlib --download

- --download fetches the pinned SmokeyNet metadata.pkl (sha256-checked) and streams the 145 FIgLib archives (about 11.4 GB), keeping only the JPEG frames.
