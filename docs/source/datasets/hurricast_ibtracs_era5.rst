Hurricast IBTrACS + ERA5 Inputs
===============================

The inputs of Hurricast (Boussioux et al. 2022): 30 IBTrACS statistical features and nine storm-centred ERA5 maps per 3-hour step, 8 steps per sample, with the 24-hour intensity or position as target.

Overview
--------

``hurricast_ibtracs_era5`` builds Hurricast's samples. From IBTrACS (``read_ibtracs``) it keeps the rows on the 3-hour grid, interpolates the WMO wind and pressure linearly within each storm, and computes per step the 30 features of the official feature tables: latitude, longitude, WMO wind and pressure, distance to land, translation speed, cosine / sine of the day of year, direction, latitude and longitude, the Saffir-Simpson category value, one-hot basin and storm nature, and the latitude / longitude change since the previous step.

Storms are selected like the official ``sort_storm``: kept when more than ``min_steps`` (20, i.e. 60 h) of their steps reach ``min_wind`` (34 kt), cut to start at the first such step and to at most ``max_steps`` (120) steps. Each window of 8 steps followed by 8 more gives a sample (windows whose target period has a step without any movement are dropped, as the official ``remove_zeros``). Winds of samples outside the North Atlantic and Eastern Pacific are converted from 10-minute to 1-minute values (/ 0.93).

With ``era5=`` (a netCDF / zarr path or an ``xarray.Dataset`` with u, v and z, e.g. a Copernicus Climate Data Store download or the NCAR RDA copy) each step gets the nine maps u, v, z at 225, 500 and 700 hPa on the 25 x 25 one-degree grid centred on the grid point nearest to the storm (a 0.25-degree source is sampled at whole degrees). Maps are read only for samples of a split.

Data Characteristics
--------------------

- Inputs per split: ``x_stat`` ``(N, 8, 30)``, ``x_viz`` ``(N, 8, 9, 25, 25)`` (channels u225, u500, u700, v..., z...; rows north to south) and ``position`` ``(N, 2)`` (latitude, longitude at the forecast time). Targets: ``target="intensity"`` the WMO wind 24 h ahead (1-minute kt, ``tc.intensity``); ``target="displacement"`` the position 24 h ahead ``(N, 1, 2)`` (``tc.track_intensity``).
- ``standardize=True`` standardises latitude, longitude, wind, pressure, distance to land, speed and the displacements (paper Table 2) and every map channel with the training split's statistics (recorded in the metadata); cyclic encodings and categories are left as they are.
- Splits by season (paper defaults: 1980-2011 / 2012-2015 / 2016-2019); the test split keeps only ``test_basins`` (NA, EP). The paper further restricts its test cases to those with ATCF operational forecasts (837 EP / 899 NA track cases), which needs the ATCF data and is not done here.

PyHazards Usage
---------------

Fit ``hurricast`` with ``model.fit(train.inputs, train.targets, val.inputs, val.targets)`` and score it with the ``tc`` benchmark: ``tc.intensity`` (MAE in kt) or ``tc.track_intensity`` with lead 24 h (great-circle error in km, the paper's haversine metric).

Registry Workflow
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Primary dataset name: ``hurricast_ibtracs_era5``

.. code-block:: python

   from pyhazards.datasets import load_dataset

   data = load_dataset(
       "hurricast_ibtracs_era5",
       path="ibtracs.since1980.list.v04r01.csv",
       era5="era5_u_v_z_225_500_700.nc",   # covering the storms' 25 x 25 degree neighbourhoods
       target="intensity",
   ).load()
   train = data.get_split("train")
   print(train.inputs["x_stat"].shape, train.inputs["x_viz"].shape, train.targets.shape)

- path= is an IBTrACS v04 CSV or netCDF file; era5= is optional (without it only the statistical inputs are built, enough for HUML-(stat, xgb)).
- min_wind, min_steps, max_steps, window_size, predict_at, the split seasons, test_basins and basins can be changed; the defaults are the paper's.
- The official per-step CDS requests (area latitude +-12.008, longitude +-12, 1-degree grid) are not reproduced; the maps are cut from the data the user provides.

Notes
-----

- The official repository (no licence) did not release the code that built the paper's 30-feature tables, only their column names; the features are computed from the paper (Table 2, footnote 2) and those names, and the storm selection, interpolation, category and displacement columns are checked against the released src/utils/data_processing.py. Differences from that code: rows off the 3-hour grid (e.g. landfall records at 10:15) are dropped, WMO values are interpolated within each storm (the official numeric_data interpolates across the whole table, so a storm's first gaps take values from the previous storm), and spur tracks are skipped.
- Neither file is redistributed: IBTrACS follows the WDC-Meteorology open-access policy, ERA5 is CC BY 4.0 (Copernicus). The tests use the 4-storm IBTrACS cut of tests/fixtures/ibtracs and a 9-time ERA5 cut around Hurricane Ophelia (tests/fixtures/era5).
