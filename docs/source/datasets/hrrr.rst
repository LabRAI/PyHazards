HRRR
====

NOAA's 3 km, hourly updated, convection-allowing forecast model for the United States, archived on AWS since July 2014.

Overview
--------

The High-Resolution Rapid Refresh is NOAA's operational convection-allowing model. It is run every hour and produces short-range forecasts of near-surface and upper-air weather at 3 km.

Its near-surface temperature, humidity, wind and precipitation forecasts are a common source of short-range fire-weather inputs; since HRRRv4 it also predicts wildfire smoke inline.

Data Characteristics
--------------------

- Forecast length changed with the model version: 15 hours until 2016, 18 hours from about September 2016, 36 hours at the synoptic cycles from mid-2018 and 48 hours from December 2020.
- Files are grouped into surface (wrfsfc), pressure-level (wrfprs), native-level (wrfnat) and sub-hourly (wrfsubh) products; AWS keys look like hrrr.YYYYMMDD/conus/hrrr.tHHz.wrfsfcfFF.grib2.
- The first two months on AWS predate operations and some early surface files are missing.

Typical Use Cases
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

- Short-range fire-weather covariates (wind, humidity, temperature) for wildfire models.
- Smoke forecast fields from HRRRv4 onwards.
