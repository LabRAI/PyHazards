GeoMAC Historical Perimeters
============================

Wildfire perimeters processed by the USGS GeoMAC service from 2000 to 2019, now distributed by the National Interagency Fire Center.

Overview
--------

GeoMAC was the USGS-operated wildfire mapping website (2000-2020) that published fire perimeters for incident support. After it was shut down on 30 April 2020, its historical perimeters moved to the National Interagency Fire Center (NIFC) open-data site.

It is the main national perimeter archive for 2000-2019. For 2021 onwards the interagency WFIGS perimeters are the successor; the NIFC Interagency Fire Perimeter History layer also covers perimeters through 2019.

Data Characteristics
--------------------

- The combined 2000-2018 layer holds 23,776 perimeters; 2019 is a separate layer.
- Attributes include incidentname, fireyear, perimeterdatetime, gisacres, agency, mapmethod, uniquefireidentifier, irwinid, state and complexname.
- The perimeters are the last ones processed by the GeoMAC team for each fire; NIFC notes that they are not necessarily the final or official perimeter.

Typical Use Cases
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

- National wildfire burned-area footprints for 2000-2019.
- Extending perimeter records before the WFIGS era.
