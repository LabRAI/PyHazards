SNODAS
======

NOAA NOHRSC's daily 1 km snow analysis (snow water equivalent, depth, melt, sublimation) for the conterminous United States, distributed by NSIDC since 2003.

Overview
--------

SNODAS is a modelling and data-assimilation system developed by NOHRSC to provide the best possible daily estimates of snow cover and related variables on a 1 km grid, in support of hydrologic modelling and analysis.

For hazard work it describes snowpack and melt timing, which shape spring flood risk and the length of the fire season in snow-dominated mountains.

Data Characteristics
--------------------

- Eight variables: snow water equivalent, snow depth, snowmelt runoff, snowpack sublimation, blowing-snow sublimation, solid and liquid precipitation, and snowpack average temperature.
- Known defects documented by NSIDC: snow water equivalent and depth wrongly zero in 126,950 cells from 9 October 2014 to 10 October 2019 (a repair mask is provided), values saturating at 32.767 m, snowpack temperature missing before 21 February 2004 and a 5% cell shift on 1 October 2013.
- The unmasked version's coverage is not consistent until 2013.

Typical Use Cases
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

- Snowmelt inputs for spring flood and streamflow models.
- Snow-cover duration and melt timing as covariates of mountain fire seasons.
