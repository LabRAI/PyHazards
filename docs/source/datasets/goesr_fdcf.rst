GOES-R ABI Fire/Hot Spot Characterization (FDCF)
================================================

NOAA's GOES-R ABI Level-2 fire detection and characterization product on the full-disk scan, every 10 minutes since April 2019, openly distributed on AWS.

Overview
--------

ABI-L2-FDCF is the full-disk version of the GOES-R Fire/Hot Spot Characterization product. The FDCA algorithm (the operational version of WFABBA) flags fire pixels in Advanced Baseline Imager data and estimates their fire radiative power, temperature and area.

It gives geostationary fire activity every 10 minutes, much more often than the polar-orbiting MODIS and VIIRS detections distributed by FIRMS, at a coarser 2 km pixel.

Data Characteristics
--------------------

- Variables: Mask (fire category codes, e.g. 10 good-quality fire, 11 saturated, 12 cloud contaminated, 13-15 high / medium / low probability, 30-35 their temporally filtered versions), Power (MW), Temp (K), Area (m2) and DQF, plus viewing angles and summaries.
- Pixels are on the ABI fixed grid (geostationary projection) and need reprojection for latitude-longitude work.
- AWS buckets per satellite: noaa-goes16 (2017-05-24 to 2025-04-07), noaa-goes17 (2018-08-27 to January 2023), noaa-goes18 (from 2022-05-11) and noaa-goes19 (from 2024-10-17); GOES-East data after April 2025 are in noaa-goes19. Keys look like ABI-L2-FDCF/YYYY/DDD/HH/OR_ABI-L2-FDCF-M6_G16_s....nc.

Typical Use Cases
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

- High-frequency fire activity time series over the Americas.
- Fire radiative power for growth and intensity analyses.
- Timing ignitions more precisely than polar-orbiting detections allow.
