LandScan Global
===============

ORNL's annual 30 arc-second global grid of ambient (24-hour average) population, an exposure layer for hazard impact studies.

Overview
--------

LandScan Global estimates the ambient (24-hour average) population of every 30 arc-second cell of the world, as one grid per year from 2000.

For hazards it is a population-exposure layer: how many people live or spend time where a fire, flood or storm occurs.

Data Characteristics
--------------------

- One band of population counts per cell, one file per year.
- Ambient (24-hour average) population rather than night-time residence counts.
- Downloads from the ORNL portal require a short registration form; the 2000-2022 releases are also mirrored on figshare.

Typical Use Cases
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

- Population exposure to wildfire, flood or tropical-cyclone footprints.
- Weighting hazard maps by the people affected.
