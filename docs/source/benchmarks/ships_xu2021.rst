SHIPS Predictors (Xu et al. 2021)
=================================

Overview
--------

``ships_xu2021`` reads the authors' table (Zenodo 4784610, CC BY 4.0): 121 SHIPS predictors per storm and forecast time and the 24-hour change of the maximum wind in knots. Test rows are a year's Atlantic operational cases; the paper averages the folds 2010-2018.
