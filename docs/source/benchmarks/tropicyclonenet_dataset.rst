TropiCycloneNet Dataset
=======================

Overview
--------

``tropicyclonenet_dataset`` reads the TCND files (Zenodo, CC BY 4.0) into TropiCycloneNet's input layout; targets are position, central pressure (hPa) and maximum wind (m/s) at 6, 12, 18 and 24 h.

The paper reports best-of-6 errors (per lead time and variable, the smallest error among six sampled forecasts); the evaluator reports those and the errors of the sample mean.
